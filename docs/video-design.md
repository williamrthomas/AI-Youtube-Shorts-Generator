# Video design

Clean New England editorial design. Not luxury-agent gloss, not social-media
sticker soup.

## Palette

| Role | Coastal | Mountain | Historic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#0F2A43` deep navy | `#1B2A22` | `#2B2118` |
| Paper | `#F6F1E4` warm cream | `#F3EFE6` | `#F5EEDF` |
| Accent | `#7FA99B` sea glass | `#5C7A66` | `#8A6A3C` |
| Secondary | `#A6402F` weathered red | `#B4643C` | `#7B2E28` |
| Muted | `#6E7B85` granite | `#77807A` | `#7A7266` |

One display serif for headlines, one highly legible sans for facts.

## Structure of a seven-minute episode

| Section | Target | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Cold open | 10–15s | Strongest single image, state the promise |
| Intro | 15–20s | Criteria and the date facts were checked |
| Property 5 → 1 | 45–75s each | Countdown, strongest story last |
| Comparison / close | 20–35s | Ask a specific viewer choice |

## Chapter pattern

Reveal (rank, town, price) → visual identity → concrete facts → experience →
one local-context beat *only if evidence supports it* → the tradeoff →
broker credit and a varied transition.

## Motion

Subtle Ken Burns, capped at 1.25× zoom, direction alternating so movement never
feels mechanical. Crossfades at 0.5s; cuts where the plan asks for one. No
stretching, no fake 3D, no transition packs competing with the properties. A
visual change at least every 6–10 seconds; no image held longer than 12s.

## On-screen rules

- Everything inside a 5% safe margin.
- Chapter cards carry rank, town, price, key facts and a small source line.
- Selective kinetic text for prices and place names — never every spoken word.
- Required disclosures: "Prices and availability checked <date>", price and
  town, listing brokerage credit, "approx." wherever the source is approximate,
  and seasonal/condo/land-lease/pending status when the template allows it.

## Audio

Narration normalized, music ducked beneath it, integrated loudness ≈ −14 LUFS
with true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Music enters only from the operator's local library
and only with a `.license.json` record beside it — no record, no use.

## Thumbnails

Three 1280×720 variants, each built from an image of a property that is
actually in the episode. Five words maximum, price legible at 10% scale, text
inside the safe area, and a legibility report per variant.
